# Settings

Settings is in the sidebar of Dictate's window, under History. Cmd+, from the menu bar menu opens it on Setup. Setup shows a badge until its steps are done.

- Setup: the steps to get going. [Activate Dictate](./activation.md), allow the three permissions and download the speech model. Below them are Open at login and the Data retention settings: Keep audio recordings (until transcribed, 1 day or 7 days, and forever in Developer mode) and Keep history (7 days, 30 days or forever).
- Shortcuts: one for dictation and one for calls. A switch sets whether Dictate asks to record calls (on by default).
- Microphones: the default microphone for dictation and for online calls. Call audio is the app (or Blend Mic mix) whose sound is the other side of a call. Cancel echo from the call is on by default. It keeps the call out of Me when it plays on speakers. If the chosen microphone isn't connected, the default one records and Dictate says so.
- Model: the speech model is Large v3 turbo (recommended) or Large v3 (3.1 GB), which does better in some languages. It can't change during an online call. The language is always detected. Below it are the retry switch with its numbers and your key terms with Dictate's suggestions. The Intelligence model serves two switches. Fix the text before pasting starts off. Add a title to each call automatically starts on. Apple Intelligence is the default. The picker also lists Ollama's models and says when either can't be used. The key terms go to Whisper whether or not Fix the text before pasting is on.
- Agents: the main switch (off until you turn it on), the server's host and port and the token. Below them are the setup to paste into an agent app and a switch for each tool. Tools that stop a recording or change stored data start off.
- About: the version, your activation ID, Send Feedback, updates and what's new in each version. Developer mode adds the Advanced pane. The third-party notices are at the bottom.

Dictate asks on its second launch whether to check for updates automatically. Updates are signed, and Dictate installs only ones signed with its own key.
