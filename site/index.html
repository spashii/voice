Dictate anywhere

Option+` starts and stops. The words appear while you speak and are pasted where you were typing. Nothing is lost, even in a crash.

Calls, both sides

With a Blend mix, Voice transcribes you and the call separately and live, as Me and Call.

Your agents read it

Claude Code and other agents search dictations and follow a live call over MCP, with a token.

Your audio never leaves your Mac: no accounts, no analytics. Voice goes online only to download the speech model and to check for updates.
