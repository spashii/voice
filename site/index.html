I wanted really fast transcription on my own Whisper pipeline, one I could hack on. Handy and OpenSuperWhisper are my favorites and I learned a lot from them, but I wanted something simpler. With Blend, Voice also hears both sides of an online call, so it writes down you and them separately.

Online calls, both sides apart

With a Blend split mix, you're Me and everyone else is Call. Voice asks when an online call starts, or press Option+Shift+`.

Your agents read along

Claude Code and other agents follow an online call live and search past ones over MCP.

No key throws a recording away

The audio is on disk as you speak. After a crash or a quit, the next launch transcribes it.

Dictation, and how it works

Press Option+` in any text field, talk, and press it again. Whisper large v3 turbo runs on your Mac with your key terms in its prompt, and Voice decodes the segments it's unsure of a second time and shows you what each retry changed.

Rough edges: Apple silicon only. Both sides of an online call need Blend. The speech model is a 1.6 GB download.

Your audio never leaves your Mac, and there are no accounts. Voice goes online only to download the speech model and to check for updates. Update checks and downloads go through apps.idea2prod.in, which counts them by app, version and country and never stores your IP address.
